About Explore Uganda

Promoting Uganda's tourism to the world, one destination at a time.

Who We Are

Introducing Explore Uganda

Explore Uganda is an online tourism guide created to introduce local and international visitors to Uganda's beautiful destinations, wildlife, cultural heritage and adventure activities.

Whether you are a student on an educational trip, a family on holiday or an adventurer looking for the next thrill, this website helps you plan and enjoy your journey.

Our Mission

To promote Uganda as a leading tourism destination by sharing accurate, inspiring and useful travel information.

Our Vision

A Uganda where every citizen and visitor appreciates and protects the country's natural and cultural treasures.

Our Objectives

  1. Showcase Uganda's top tourist destinations.
  2. Educate visitors about Ugandan culture.
  3. Encourage domestic tourism among Ugandans.
  4. Promote responsible and sustainable travel.
History

Why Uganda is called the Pearl of Africa

In 1908, Winston Churchill visited Uganda and described it in his book My African Journey as "the Pearl of Africa". He was amazed by its variety of landscapes, abundant wildlife, colourful birds and friendly people.

Today the name still fits: Uganda combines the best of Africa – savannah, rainforest, snow-capped mountains and great lakes – in one country.

Impact

Importance of Tourism to Uganda

  • Tourism is one of Uganda's leading sources of foreign exchange.
  • It creates jobs for guides, hotel workers, drivers and craft makers.
  • Park fees support wildlife conservation and community projects.
  • It helps preserve and celebrate traditional culture.
  • It encourages the building of roads and other infrastructure.
