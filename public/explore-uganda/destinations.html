Where to Go

Destinations in Uganda

National parks, natural wonders and cultural sites from every region of the country.

National Parks

Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

📍 South-western Uganda (Kanungu)

An ancient rainforest and UNESCO World Heritage Site, famous for mountain gorillas.

  • Gorilla trekking
  • Nature walks
  • Batwa experience
Learn More

Murchison Falls National Park

📍 North-western Uganda (Masindi)

Uganda's largest park, where the Nile explodes through a narrow gorge.

  • Game drives
  • Boat cruise
  • Hike to the top of the falls
Learn More

Queen Elizabeth National Park

📍 Western Uganda (Kasese)

Savannah, crater lakes and the Kazinga Channel, with tree-climbing lions in Ishasha.

  • Game drives
  • Kazinga boat cruise
  • Chimp tracking
Learn More

Kidepo Valley National Park

📍 North-eastern Uganda (Kaabong)

A remote, rugged wilderness with ostriches, cheetahs and stunning valleys.

  • Game drives
  • Karamojong visits
  • Hiking
Learn More

Lake Mburo National Park

📍 Western Uganda (Kiruhura)

The closest savannah park to Kampala, home to zebras, impalas and elands.

  • Horseback safari
  • Boat trips
  • Walking safari
Learn More

Kibale National Park

📍 Western Uganda (Fort Portal)

The primate capital of the world, with about 13 primate species including chimpanzees.

  • Chimpanzee tracking
  • Bigodi swamp walk
  • Birding
Learn More

Natural Attractions

Lake Bunyonyi

📍 South-western Uganda (Kabale)

One of Africa's deepest lakes, dotted with 29 islands and terraced hills.

  • Canoeing
  • Swimming
  • Island hopping
Learn More

Rwenzori Mountains

📍 Western Uganda (Kasese)

The 'Mountains of the Moon', with Africa's third-highest peak.

  • Mountain climbing
  • Hiking
  • Bakonzo culture
Learn More

Sipi Falls

📍 Eastern Uganda (Kapchorwa)

Three beautiful waterfalls on the slopes of Mount Elgon, surrounded by coffee farms.

  • Abseiling
  • Coffee tours
  • Hiking
Learn More

Source of the Nile

📍 Eastern Uganda (Jinja)

The point where the Nile begins its 6,650 km journey to the Mediterranean.

  • Rafting
  • Kayaking
  • Sunset cruise
Learn More

Lake Victoria

📍 Central Uganda (Entebbe)

Africa's largest lake, with sandy beaches and the Ssese Islands.

  • Beaches
  • Fishing
  • Ngamba chimp island
Learn More

Cultural and Historical Attractions

Kasubi Tombs

📍 Central Uganda (Kampala)

UNESCO World Heritage Site and burial ground of the Kabakas of Buganda.

  • Heritage tour
  • Architecture
  • History
Learn More

Uganda Museum

📍 Central Uganda (Kampala)

East Africa's oldest museum, founded in 1908, with cultural and natural history exhibits.

  • Exhibitions
  • Music instruments
  • Education

Kabaka's Palace (Lubiri)

  • Royal history
  • Photography
