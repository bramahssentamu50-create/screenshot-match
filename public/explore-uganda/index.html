Why Uganda?

A country that has it all

Uganda sits at the heart of East Africa where the savannah meets the rainforest. In one journey you can track gorillas, see lions on a game drive and stand where the Nile begins.

  • Natural beauty: lakes, mountains, waterfalls and green hills.
  • Wildlife: the Big Five, mountain gorillas, chimpanzees and over 1,000 bird species.
  • Cultural diversity: more than 50 ethnic groups and historic kingdoms.
  • Adventure: white-water rafting, hiking, bungee jumping and kayaking.
  • Hospitality: Ugandans are known for their warm smiles and welcome.

Things to Do

Tourism Experiences

🦁

Wildlife Safaris

Game drives in Queen Elizabeth, Murchison Falls and Kidepo to see lions, elephants, buffaloes and giraffes.

🦍

Gorilla Trekking

Hike through Bwindi or Mgahinga for a life-changing hour with a mountain gorilla family.

⛰

Mountain Climbing

Climb the Rwenzoris, Mount Elgon or the Virunga volcanoes for spectacular views.

🥁

Cultural Tourism

Visit kingdoms, royal tombs, craft markets and enjoy traditional music and dance.

🦅

Bird Watching

Spot the rare shoebill stork and over 1,000 other bird species.

🚣

Water Adventures

Raft the Nile, kayak, take boat cruises or canoe between Lake Bunyonyi's islands.
