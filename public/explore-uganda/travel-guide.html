Uganda Travel Guide

Everything you need to know before you go.

Best Times to Visit

Uganda can be visited all year round. The dry seasons are best for gorilla trekking and safaris:

  • December – February
  • June – August

The rainy months (March–May, September–November) are green and quieter, and great for birding.

Transportation Options

  • Fly into Entebbe International Airport.
  • Hire a safari vehicle with a driver-guide.
  • Domestic flights to Kidepo, Kihihi and Kasese.
  • Buses and coaches between major towns.
  • Boda-bodas (motorbike taxis) for short trips – always wear a helmet.

Travel Preparation

  • Check visa requirements (e-visa available online).
  • Carry a yellow fever vaccination certificate.
  • Book gorilla permits early.
  • Pack light layers, rain jacket and hiking boots.
  • Bring insect repellent and sunscreen.

Safety Advice

Follow your guide's instructions in parks, keep valuables secure, use registered transport and drink bottled or boiled water.

🏨

Accommodation

Choose from luxury lodges, mid-range hotels, budget guesthouses, eco-camps and campsites inside or near the parks.

🌿

Responsible Tourism

Respect wildlife distances, avoid plastic waste, buy local crafts, and ask permission before photographing people.

⭐

Suggested Experiences

Combine gorilla trekking in Bwindi, a Kazinga Channel cruise, a night at Lake Bunyonyi and rafting in Jinja.
